# Third-party projects

AE2 NEI AutoCraft Bridge is an independent, unofficial addon. It contains its own
bridge and ASM transformer classes; it does not bundle the following mods or
their libraries. The MIT license in this repository applies to the bridge's own
code, not to these separate projects.

- [Applied Energistics 2, GTNH fork](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/Applied-Energistics-2-Unofficial):
  [LGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/Applied-Energistics-2-Unofficial/blob/rv3-beta-1082-GTNH/LICENSE.txt).
- [NotEnoughItems, GTNH fork](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/NotEnoughItems):
  [LGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/NotEnoughItems/blob/2.8.145-GTNH/LICENSE.txt).
- [NotEnoughEnergistics](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/NotEnoughEnergistics):
  [MIT](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/NotEnoughEnergistics/blob/master/LICENSE).

Thanks to these projects and their contributors for the original crafting,
recipe-chain, and terminal integration implementations.
